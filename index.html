How I got here

Nobody handed me a playbook. So I wrote one.

This isn't a course from somebody who read about service businesses. I run one. Every mistake below cost me real money, and every fix is still working today.

  1. $12 / hr

    Raking leaves on a landscaping crew

    That was the paycheck. I did the work, watched how the owner ran things, and started saving whatever I could.

  2. $700

    Started a detailing business with my brother

    Bought the basics, printed nothing, and went and found cars to clean. No investors, no loan, no plan past the next job.

  3. The hits

    Burned by an agency. Betrayed by a worker.

    I paid a marketing agency that didn't deliver. Then someone I trusted on the crew went behind my back. Three years in, I almost called it.

  4. The turn

    Started posting the work myself

    Phone in hand, on real jobs, no production. That's what finally made the phone ring.

  5. Today

    Six workers, two trucks, two trailers — and a 2025 Raptor in the driveway

    $35–45k a month in a market as competitive as Savannah, with accounts like Chipotle and Chick-fil-A on the route. I don't work in the actual detailing anymore. I built the systems so the business runs, and now I help other service businesses do the same.

None of it was luck. It was a sequence of decisions, and I can walk you through every one.
