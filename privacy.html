Last updated November 22, 2026

Scale With Felipe ("we," "us") is operated by Siestr LLC, based in Savannah, Georgia. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us through our website form or our Facebook and Instagram lead forms: your name, phone number, email, business type, revenue range, and anything else you choose to share.

How we use your information

  • To follow up on your request, answer your questions and schedule calls.

SMS opt-in and text messaging

You opt in to text messages from Scale With Felipe (Siestr LLC) by checking the optional SMS consent box on our website form, or by submitting our Facebook/Instagram lead form, which shows the text message consent disclosure before you tap Submit. Messages include follow-ups on your request, scheduling messages, and occasional messages about our services. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help.

No sharing of SMS opt-in data: We do not sell, rent or share your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in information with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except with the service providers that help us send the messages (such as our CRM and messaging platform), who may use it only to provide that service to us.

Cookies and tracking

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies, including the Meta Pixel, to understand how visitors use the site and to measure and improve our ads. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can manage ad preferences in your Facebook/Instagram settings.

Sharing

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run our business (such as hosting, CRM, scheduling and messaging tools) under obligations to protect it, or if required by law.

Data security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect your information, including access limited to the people who need it and reputable, secured service providers. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.
